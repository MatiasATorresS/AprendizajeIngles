# Pruebas inicial y final: borradores para revisión docente

**No aplicar sin revisión docente.** Cada forma tiene 20 ítems: 10 de *Simple Past* y 10 de *Present Perfect*. Dentro de cada contenido, los primeros cinco evalúan reconocimiento de forma y los siguientes cinco, uso en contexto. Un punto por respuesta correcta; sin penalización por error. No mostrar la clave al alumnado.

## Forma A: prueba inicial

### Simple Past: forma

1. Choose the past form of **walk**. A) walks B) walked C) walking D) walk
2. Choose the past form of **go**. A) goed B) going C) went D) goes
3. Complete: “I ___ watch TV yesterday.” A) don't B) didn't C) doesn't D) wasn't
4. Complete: “___ she visit her grandmother last Sunday?” A) Does B) Is C) Did D) Has
5. Complete: “They ___ at school yesterday.” A) was B) are C) be D) were

### Simple Past: contexto

6. “Last night, we ___ a movie.” A) watch B) watches C) watched D) watching
7. “Two days ago, he ___ his keys.” A) loses B) lost C) lose D) losing
8. “Yesterday, I ___ breakfast at 8 a.m.” A) have B) has C) having D) had
9. “Did you call Ana?” “Yes, I ___.” A) do B) did C) have D) was
10. “It rained, so we ___ at home.” A) stayed B) stay C) stays D) staying

### Present Perfect: forma

11. Complete: “She ___ finished her homework.” A) have B) has C) is D) did
12. Choose the past participle of **write**. A) wrote B) writing C) written D) writes
13. Complete: “They have ___ arrived.” A) yet B) already C) ago D) yesterday
14. Complete: “I ___ seen that film.” A) hasn't B) haven't C) didn't D) wasn't
15. Complete: “___ you ever eaten sushi?” A) Did B) Do C) Have D) Has

### Present Perfect: contexto

16. “I have lived here ___ 2020.” A) for B) since C) ago D) during
17. “We have studied English ___ three years.” A) since B) ago C) for D) yesterday
18. “She has ___ visited Brazil.” A) never B) yesterday C) last week D) ago
19. “Have they finished?” “No, they haven't finished ___.” A) already B) ever C) yet D) since
20. “He has lost his phone, so he ___ call us now.” A) can't B) couldn't yesterday C) didn't D) won't yesterday

**Clave A:** 1 B, 2 C, 3 B, 4 C, 5 D, 6 C, 7 B, 8 D, 9 B, 10 A, 11 B, 12 C, 13 B, 14 B, 15 C, 16 B, 17 C, 18 A, 19 C, 20 A.

## Forma B: prueba final

### Simple Past: forma

1. Choose the past form of **play**. A) playing B) plays C) played D) play
2. Choose the past form of **see**. A) seen B) saw C) seeing D) sees
3. Complete: “We ___ travel last weekend.” A) don't B) doesn't C) didn't D) aren't
4. Complete: “___ they arrive on time yesterday?” A) Do B) Have C) Are D) Did
5. Complete: “He ___ tired after the game.” A) were B) is C) was D) be

### Simple Past: contexto

6. “Last Saturday, she ___ her room.” A) cleaned B) cleans C) clean D) cleaning
7. “Yesterday, they ___ a new teacher.” A) meet B) meets C) met D) meeting
8. “Last week, I ___ a letter to my friend.” A) write B) wrote C) written D) writes
9. “Did he enjoy the concert?” “Yes, he ___.” A) does B) has C) was D) did
10. “The bus was late, so we ___ to school.” A) walked B) walk C) walking D) walks

### Present Perfect: forma

11. Complete: “They ___ completed the task.” A) has B) are C) have D) did
12. Choose the past participle of **take**. A) took B) taking C) takes D) taken
13. Complete: “I have ___ sent the email.” A) yesterday B) already C) ago D) last night
14. Complete: “He ___ read that book.” A) haven't B) doesn't C) hasn't D) didn't
15. Complete: “___ she ever flown on a plane?” A) Did B) Does C) Have D) Has

### Present Perfect: contexto

16. “They have known each other ___ 2019.” A) for B) since C) ago D) yesterday
17. “I have played football ___ two years.” A) since B) ago C) for D) last
18. “We have ___ been to Japan.” A) last year B) never C) yesterday D) ago
19. “Has she called you?” “No, she hasn't called me ___.” A) already B) ever C) since D) yet
20. “She has broken her glasses, so she ___ see clearly now.” A) can't B) couldn't yesterday C) didn't D) won't yesterday

**Clave B:** 1 C, 2 B, 3 C, 4 D, 5 C, 6 A, 7 C, 8 B, 9 D, 10 A, 11 C, 12 D, 13 B, 14 C, 15 D, 16 B, 17 C, 18 B, 19 D, 20 A.

## Antes de su uso

El docente debe comprobar la corrección de cada ítem, la adecuación al curso y la equivalencia de dificultad de ambas formas con la [ficha de revisión](ficha-revision-docente.md). Tras corregirlas, guardar versiones numeradas y actualizar las claves. La equivalencia no queda demostrada solo por compartir esta tabla de especificaciones.
